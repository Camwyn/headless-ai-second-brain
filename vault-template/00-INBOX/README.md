# 00-INBOX (Frictionless Quick Capture)

> **Purpose:** A frictionless landing zone for raw ideas, unorganized voice transcripts, meeting dumps, or rough research notes.

## 📥 Rules of the Inbox:
1. **Zero Stress:** Don't worry about folders, tags, or formatting when capturing notes here.
2. **Weekly Synthesis:** Use an AI assistant during weekly reviews to process inbox notes into structured `Projects/`, `Areas/`, or `Resources/`.
3. **Keep It Lean:** Move or archive processed notes so the inbox stays clear.
