## 4. Operational Guardrails for AI Workers

1. **Atomic & Attribution-Aware Worklogs:** When recording updates, always append to the relevant project `Worklog.md` with date and author (e.g. `### YYYY-MM-DD — @assistant`).
2. **Never Overwrite Without Reading First:** Always perform an `obsidian_read_note` before modifying an existing note.
3. **Structured Frontmatter:** Always preserve and maintain YAML frontmatter (`title`, `type`, `status`, `tags`, `updated_at`).
4. **File Placement:**
   - Active, finite deliverables go to `Projects/<project-name>/`.
   - Permanent standards, brand assets, and governance rules go to `Areas/<area-name>/`.
   - General reference material goes to `Resources/<topic>/`.
   - Retired or historical records go to `Archives/`.
